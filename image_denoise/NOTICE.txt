SuperBirdTools includes an adapted, inference-only implementation of
basicsr/models/archs/NAFNet_arch.py and the LayerNorm2d calculation from
basicsr/models/archs/arch_util.py. BasicSR training utilities, custom autograd
backward functions, local-conversion code and demonstrations are omitted.
Layer normalization uses ordinary PyTorch operations; checkpoint parameter
names, FP32 calculations and the official SIDD width64 topology are retained.
